abstract: "We relate light attenuation measured along 56 transects to macrophyte cover and leaf stress indicators. Transects with a diffuse attenuation coefficient above 2.1 per metre showed a clear decline in cover the following season."

Light attenuation calculations follow standard PAR/PUR practice. An independent implementation of the light calculations is available in the open-AIMS Light_calcs project, which we used for cross-checking but which is not the source of this paper's transect data.
