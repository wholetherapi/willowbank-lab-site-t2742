title: "Retention of fine sediment in restored riparian buffers"
slug: riparian-buffer-retention
collection: publications
category: journal-article
date: 2021-06-21
venue: "Freshwater Ecology Letters, 14(3): 88-104"
status: published
authors: "V. Diaz, R. Sable, M. Okonkwo"
doi: "10.1142/fel.2021.0088"
abstract: "Ten restored buffers retained between 31 and 68 percent of the fine sediment delivered from adjacent arable fields during storm events. Retention scaled with buffer width and stem density rather than with age since restoration."
